# Valocraft game design

Valocraft is a voxel sandbox RPG built on Veloren. It aims for the warmth of a
fairy-tale adventure RPG and the freedom of a block-building sandbox:
explore a large generated world, fight its monsters, and build a home that the
world reacts to.

This is a living document. Status markers: **[done]**, **[next]**,
**[planned]**.

## Pillars

1. **The world is your material.** Any natural block can be gathered and
   placed again. Building is survival-first, with creative areas for admins.
2. **A world worth defending.** Veloren's generated towns, dungeons, factions
   and rtsim NPCs give the building a purpose and the combat a context.
3. **Readable, cheerful and expressive.** A bright palette, chunky silhouettes
   and clear feedback: the player always knows what happened and why.
4. **Respect the player's time.** Short loops (gather → craft → build) nest
   inside long ones (settle → grow a village → take on bigger threats).

## Core loops

### Moment to moment **[done]**

- Toggle build mode (`Z`), or use a block from the hotbar/bag.
- Break terrain within reach to gather block items; place them back.
- Pick a block from the world with the roll key (middle mouse).
- Rock and ice need a pickaxe. Towns, dungeons and other sites are protected.

### Session

- Gather basics (dirt, turf, sand, stone, timber), craft upgrades (planks,
  stone bricks, clay bricks) **[done]**.
- Build a shelter near resources; fight what comes at night **[planned]**.

### Long term **[planned]**

- **Rooms.** Enclosed spaces with a door and a light source are recognised as
  rooms (DQB-style idea, our own rules and implementation). Room types come
  from their furniture: bedroom, kitchen, workshop.
- **Settlers.** rtsim NPCs move into a player settlement once it has rooms
  for them, take jobs that match those rooms and ask for improvements.
- **Threats.** Raids scale with the settlement, which gives combat and
  building a shared goal.

## Art direction

References: chunky voxel characters with big, expressive faces; bright,
saturated fairy-tale palettes; cozy villages of cobblestone paths, timber
frames and tiled roofs; villains that read instantly from their silhouette
(goblins, trolls, ogres, skeleton warriors, slimes and caterpillars). These
are *genre* references only.

Rules:

- **Original or permissively licensed assets only** (CC0, CC-BY, OFL, ...),
  credited in `assets/credits.ron`. No assets ripped from, traced from or
  "inspired-copied" from Dragon Quest, Hytale or any other proprietary game,
  even for private builds.
- Characters: large heads, readable eyes, strong colour blocking; armour and
  clothing as separate layers (Veloren's modular figure system supports this).
- Villains: one dominant colour family per faction, exaggerated features
  (tusks, brows, oversized weapons), a clear "tell" before attacks.
- Blocks and items: flat colour with simple, high-contrast patterns that read
  at icon size. Block icons and drop models are generated by
  `scripts/gen_building_blocks.py`, which is deterministic and versioned.
- UI: dark translucent panels, a thin highlight accent, short text.
