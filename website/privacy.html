Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Version 1.0

TL;DR

Valocraft is offline-first. We don’t require an account for local play, we don’t sell your data, and your saves stay on your device. If you connect to a public multiplayer server, that server’s operator may see your IP address and chosen username.

What this covers

This policy applies to the Valocraft website and the Valocraft game.

Information we collect

Website

This website is static and requires no personal data to browse. If it is hosted with standard web server logs, those logs may briefly record your IP address, browser, and referrer. They are not sold or shared.

Game (local play)

Valocraft is offline-first. Local play creates no account and sends no personal data anywhere. Saves, settings, and worlds live locally in the userdata/ folder on your device.

Multiplayer

Valocraft has no central account service and runs no central player database. When you connect to a dedicated server, that server’s operator may see your IP address and chosen username. Servers with authentication run their own self-hosted auth service; their own privacy practices apply.

Cookies & analytics

This website uses no tracking cookies and no analytics.

How data is used

Any data exchanged is used only to run the game and its multiplayer connections. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Data retention

Local saves stay on your device until you delete them. Web server logs, if any, are kept only briefly.

Your rights

Your game data is on your own device: you can access, correct, export, or delete it at any time by managing the userdata/ folder.

Children

The game is general-audience and collects no personal data from children for local play.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
