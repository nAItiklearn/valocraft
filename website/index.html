Valocraft

A voxel open-world action-adventure RPG.

Explore vast procedurally generated continents, fight in real time, master weapons and magic, craft powerful gear, and build your mark on the world — alone offline or with friends on a dedicated server. No account, no login, just play.

What you can do

Explore a living world

Continents, caves, ruins, and settlements with a dynamic day/night cycle, weather, and a full world map. Climb, swim, glide, and ride tamed mounts.

Fight in real time

Swords, axes, bows, and magic staffs. Block, dodge, roll, and master weapon abilities against wildlife, hostile factions, and dungeon bosses.

Build your character

Choose your race, customize your look, earn XP, unlock skills, and tailor your build with modular weapons, armor, gliders, and lanterns.

Craft, cook, and trade

Forge weapons and armor at workbenches, brew potions, cook food, and trade with NPC merchants as you grow your recipe book.

Take Emberwatch contracts

Accept Survey, Bounty, and Courier contracts, track objectives on your map, and earn coins and standing to light the Emberwatch Beacon.

Play solo or together

Offline singleplayer with no account, or host your own dedicated server. Server owners run their own authentication — no central service.
